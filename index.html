How CenterCircle 7 Works

  • • 2 Teams (13+ Players Each): Everyone joins individually on their device into Team 1 or Team 2.
  • • 7 Letters + Circled Center: Every word must use only the 7 shown letters (you may repeat letters as many times as needed, even 10+ letter words) and must include the circled middle letter.
  • • Minimum Length: Words must be at least 3 letters long (no 2-letter words).
  • • No Proper Nouns: Names of people, cities, or brands are not allowed.
  • • Alternating Individual Turns: Turns alternate Team 1 → Team 2 → Team 1 → Team 2, rotating through a different player each time.
  • • 15-Second Clock: If time runs out, the turn passes to the next player on the other team.
  • • Scoring: 3–5 letters = 1 pt; 6+ letters = +1 pt per extra letter above 5 (6 letters = 2 pts, 7 = 3 pts, 8 = 4 pts, etc.).
